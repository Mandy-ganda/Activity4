    Which one should I use?

    • Small lists: insertion sort or bubble sort are the simplest choices.
    • Large lists: merge sort or quick sort, thanks to their O(n log n) speed.
    • Memory is tight: quick sort, because it has a smaller memory footprint than merge sort.
    • Stability needed (equal items keep their order): merge sort is stable, while the standard in-place quick sort is not.
